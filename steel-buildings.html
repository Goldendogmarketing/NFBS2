Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering a metal building in North Florida.

Every metal building from North Florida Building Solutions includes free delivery and professional installation on your prepared site. Our certified crews handle all assembly, anchoring, and final inspection. You just need a level site — we do the rest.
Most standard carports and garages are installed in 1 day. Larger commercial buildings or barns may take 2-3 days. After you order, typical lead time is 4-8 weeks depending on the season.
Permit requirements vary by county and city in North Florida. Most counties in our service area (Bradford, Clay, Alachua, Putnam, Marion, etc.) require permits for permanent structures. We can help guide you through the process and provide engineered drawings if needed.
Your site needs to be level within a few inches. Most customers install on a gravel pad, concrete slab, or compacted dirt. We recommend a concrete slab for fully enclosed garages and barns. We can add concrete as part of your order.
All of our metal buildings come with a 20-year rust-through warranty on panels and a 1-year workmanship warranty on installation. Certified steel components carry their own manufacturer warranties.
Absolutely. Choose from a wide range of panel colors, trim options, roll-up door sizes (8x8, 10x10, 12x12), walk-in doors, and window placements. Our configurator gives you a starting estimate, and we'll finalize every detail on your confirmation call.
Certified buildings are engineered to meet specific wind and snow load requirements set by your local building code. In Florida, wind certification (up to 170 mph in some areas) is often required for permitted structures. Certified buildings use heavier gauge steel and additional bracing. We'll help you determine what your county requires.
We offer rent-to-own programs with as little as 10% down and no credit check required. Traditional financing is also available. Monthly payments can be as low as $89/month depending on the building. Contact us for a personalized payment plan.
Our buildings are made from commercial-grade galvanized steel designed to withstand Florida's heat, humidity, heavy rain, and hurricanes. Vertical roof styles shed water and debris most effectively. With proper certification, our buildings meet Florida wind load requirements up to 170 mph.
It depends on the building type. Open carports can be anchored to the ground with rebar or mobile home anchors. For enclosed garages and barns, we strongly recommend a concrete slab (4-6 inches thick) for stability, moisture control, and longevity. We offer concrete slab installation as an add-on.
